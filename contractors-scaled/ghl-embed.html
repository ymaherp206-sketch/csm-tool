01 / What we build

Full remodels. The rooms that change how a home lives.

We take on the big four and take them all the way. Each one planned, priced, and built by the same team, so nothing gets lost between the drawing and the drywall.

  1. A-101

    Kitchens

    Layouts that work the way you cook, cabinetry fitted to the wall, and finishes chosen once, not three times. Plumbing, electrical, and lighting handled inside the same scope.
  2. A-102

    Bathrooms

    Primary baths, hall baths, and the ones that haven't been touched since the house was built. Tile, waterproofing, fixtures, ventilation, and storage, all planned together.
  3. A-103

    Basements

    Square footage you already own, finished into space you'll actually use. Family rooms, guest suites, home offices, and gyms, with moisture, egress, and mechanicals sorted first.
  4. A-104

    Pools

    In-ground pools designed for the yard you have, with decking, equipment, and the outdoor living around it planned as one project instead of four.

A remodel should feel like one decision you made, not a hundred you keep making.

That is the whole reason we exist. One scope, one price, one person. You decide what you want the room to be. We handle everything between that and the day it's done.

02 / How it works

Three steps. One point of contact.

You will know what is happening, what it costs, and who is doing it, at every stage.

  1. Step 01

    Plan Scope & design

    It starts with a call, then a visit to your home. We measure, listen, and turn what you want into a written scope you can actually read, down to the fixtures and finishes.

  2. Step 02

    Price One proposal

    You get one proposal that covers the whole project, priced before any work begins. If something needs to change along the way, you hear it from us first, in writing.

  3. Step 03

    Build Vetted local crew

    A vetted, licensed crew local to you builds it. You keep talking to the same person the entire time, from the first call to the final walkthrough.

Every project starts the same way: a short conversation about the room, then an in-home visit.

05 / Good questions

Things people ask before they call.

Where do you work?

Across the US. We plan and price the project, then build it with a vetted, licensed crew local to your home.

Who is actually doing the work in my house?

A local crew we have vetted and that is licensed where the work is being done. We manage them, and you deal with one point of contact throughout.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Full remodels: kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and pools. If you're looking for a small repair or a single fixture swap, we're probably not the right fit.

How does pricing work?

After the in-home visit you get one proposal covering the full scope. If anything needs to change during the build, you'll see it in writing before it happens.
